December is the month when event work moves the most. In Argentina it is the end of the year and the start of summer at the same time: company year end parties, weddings, birthdays, end of season events, brand events before the holidays. There are dates for anyone who wants them, but they get handed out fast and go to the people who are ready first. This guide is so you reach 1 December with everything sorted and get through the month without burning out.

You won't find here how many events there are or how much they pay: there is no serious source that says so and we are not going to make it up. What is in your hands is how you prepare.

## Why November is the key month

Whoever puts together the team for a year end party doesn't wait until December to look for people. They build the list earlier, and when a date comes up they write first to the people they already know about. If your profile is half done or your availability says nothing useful, you get skipped. Use November to:

- **Complete or update your profile.**
- **Think about how many dates you want to take** and which days you can't do.
- **Sort out whatever is missing**: clothes, paperwork, an account in your name to get paid.

## Your profile, up to date before December

On LABURO you can edit your profile from your account whenever you want. For the season, what matters most:

- **The roles you apply for**: mark the ones you can do, not all of them. A profile that ticks everything says very little.
- **Where you want to work**: the areas that really suit you, thinking about how you get home at night.
- **Available at weekends**: if you are, tick it. Many parties fall on Fridays and Saturdays, although in December weekdays fill up too.
- **How much notice you need to take a job**: if you can accept at short notice, say so. In peak season some dates get covered from one day to the next, when someone drops out.
- **Your experience**: roles and type of event, with dates. If you have already worked at company parties or weddings, make it visible.

What to write and what not to, with examples, is in [what to include in your event staff profile](/en/blog/event-staff-profile-what-to-include).

## Keep your own calendar

In December the most common mistake is not running out of dates: it is accepting too many. Before you say yes:

- **Write down every date you accept** with arrival time, finish time and address.
- **Check how long it takes** to get from one place to the other if you have two things on the same day.
- **Leave a margin**: year end events run late. If you finish at 2 a.m., don't accept something at 8.
- **Count the days in a row.** Five nights on your feet back to back take their toll.

## The terms, in writing

In the rush of the season it is easy to accept over WhatsApp without asking anything. Before confirming, make sure it is clear:

- **The pay** and when you get paid.
- **Start and finish times**, and what happens if the event runs over.
- **What clothes they ask for.** At year end parties it is often black or formal.
- **Whether there is food** during the shift.
- **How you get home** if it ends in the early hours. For that, see [getting home after a late night event](/en/blog/getting-home-after-a-late-night-event).

## If you can't make it, give notice in time

In December everyone is tired and sometimes a date falls through on your side. It happens. What separates the people who stay on the list from those who don't is how they let people know: as early as possible, through the same channel where they accepted, and without disappearing. How to do it properly is in [how to cancel an event shift](/en/blog/how-to-cancel-an-event-shift).

## Look after yourself during the month

- **Sleep when you can**, even during the day.
- **Drink water.** December is hot in Argentina and many events are outdoors or in packed venues.
- **Comfortable shoes** that also meet what they ask for.
- **Eat before you go in** if you don't know whether there will be food.

## December is the door to summer

The dates you do well in December are the ones that get you called again in January and February. Arriving on time, doing what was asked and staying until the end count for more than anything you put on your profile.
